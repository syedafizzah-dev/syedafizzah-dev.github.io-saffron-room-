A café that keeps its voice low and its standards high.

The Saffron Room opened with one idea: a place where the coffee is carefully sourced, the pastry is baked before sunrise, and nobody rushes you.

We roast in small batches, infuse our signature syrup with Kashmiri saffron, and set every table with linen, brass and fresh flowers. Come for a quiet espresso, stay for a long brunch.

  • Small-batch roasting Single-origin beans, roasted weekly.
  • Baked daily Every pastry is made in our kitchen each morning.
  • Private corners Booths for two, a long table for ten.
